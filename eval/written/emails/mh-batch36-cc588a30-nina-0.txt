Hi Nina,

You picked “Traffic but no sales.” I took a close look at your two key pages — Home and Free Valuation — where most of that traffic is supposed to turn into enquiries. Your whole funnel depends on the instant guide price in ~60 seconds plus a quick human follow‑up. When that feels slow, unclear, or too nosy too soon, people drop.

Below are three fixes you can ship today (with copy you can paste), plus two quick checks to confirm if the page — not the traffic — is the leak.

1) Say exactly what happens and when (remove uncertainty)
What’s on the page now mixes “instant,” “60 seconds,” “we’ll email,” and “we’ll call within 24 hours/one working day.” That reads fast-but-vague. Make the path explicit and consistent across Home and Free Valuation.

Paste-ready copy
- Headline: Get your instant guide price in about 60 seconds
- Subhead: We’ll show your price range on‑screen and email a copy. No obligation.
- CTA (primary): Get my instant valuation
- What happens next (3 bullets, directly under the button):
  • Enter your postcode and property details
  • We run local comps and give you a guide price in ~60 seconds
  • We’ll email the details; we’ll only call if we need more info or you request a call
- Trust line (move this right under the CTA): Trusted by 3,200+ local sellers and landlords • 4.7★ Reviews
- Consent microcopy (beside the checkbox): We’ll email your guide price and a short follow‑up about your move. You can opt out anytime. (Keep your Privacy and Terms links visible; your ICO number is already in the footer.)

If the tool can’t show the guide price on‑screen, replace the subhead with: We’ll email your guide price in ~60 seconds and confirm by phone only if needed. The key is to pick one promise and use it everywhere (Home, Free Valuation, callback form).

2) Reduce “work before reward” (make it a 2‑step)
Right now you ask for full contact details before any payoff. Split the flow:
- Step 1 (Your property): Postcode, property type, bedrooms, intent (Sell/Let), timeframe
- Step 2 (About you): First name, last name, email, mobile (optional), consent
Add a small progress label (“Step 1 of 2”) and auto‑advance. This lowers perceived effort and keeps people moving. If the valuation tool needs contact details to generate, still show a fast “Calculating your guide price…” screen so it feels instant.

3) Remove perceived slowness
“Loading valuation tool…” signals wait time at the exact moment you want action. Reserve the space, show a skeleton, and let people start with the postcode field immediately. Target a snappy first input under ~2 seconds and a visible result (or clear confirmation of when it arrives) in ~60 seconds.

Quick checks to run today
- Five‑second test in your analytics: Look at paid sessions landing on /free‑valuation. If roughly half leave within 5 seconds, it’s the page, not the audience. Then ship the copy + 2‑step.
- Secret shop on mobile (your real funnel):
  • Time to first input (should feel instant)
  • Time to “price” (on‑screen or email receipt) — is it actually ~60 seconds?
  • Do you say “within 24 hours” in one place and “within one working day” in another? Pick one.
  • Do you place the “Trusted by 3,200+… • 4.7★ Reviews” line right by the CTA on the valuation page (not just the home page)?

Small but high‑leverage tweaks
- Match the ad/query in the headline for paid traffic: “Leamington Spa house valuation in 60 seconds” / “Warwick” / “Kenilworth.”
- Keep “mobile” optional on Step 2; it’s often the top abandonment field in this kind of form.
- If you keep a callback option, make it explicit opt‑in (“Request a call within one working day”) instead of implied.

If it helps, forward this to whoever edits your site — it’s a copy‑paste and a simple step split.
