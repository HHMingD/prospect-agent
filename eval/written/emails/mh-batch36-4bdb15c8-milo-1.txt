What changes (practical, week-one moves)

- Keep the fallback live: Sundrift’s site threw 502s yesterday. A lightweight, single-offer fallback page on a separate host/subdomain is still the safety net for paid/social/PR.

January drinks structure (built for GEM’s broader context)
- Anchor to one campaign idea that sells an occasion/emotion across channels (wellness reset, energy/focus, sociability), not ingredients. This is how the strongest UK DTC drinks launches have won this year: one idea across TV, social, creators, retail media, and email.
- If you’re in functional/alt/premium RTD territory, bake in subscribe-and-save or loyalty from day one; hybrid DTC+retail launches are winning repeat on infrastructure, not promotions.

Ad-to-offer mapping (three-idea kit you can run)
For each of these, keep placements open and let GEM route — you control creative truth + landing intent.
1) Wellness reset
  - Assets: 9:16 UGC morning routine; 1:1 static pack + “feel X, not Y” promise; 4:5 short demo (pour/pack). 
  - Page: Plain offer page with a single CTA, brief Who/When/How, 2–3 tight proof points, and FAQs (ingredients, delivery, returns). Add a simple subscribe-and-save variant.
2) Energy/focus
  - Assets: Creator POV “before/after my 3 p.m. slump”; 6–10s product-in-use; static with benefit headline.
  - Page: Proof-heavy variant: lead with the benefit, layer credible reviews, transparent FAQs, and any credible evidence you can publish. Keep it single-path to checkout.
3) Sociability
  - Assets: Creator “mini-hosting kit” reel; UGC cheers moment; 1:1 static multipack visual.
  - Page: Chooser variant if you truly have meaningful options (pack sizes/flavours), but still one page. If not, stick to a frictionless multipack offer.

Testing order (fits a ~£120k/mo envelope without fighting GEM)
- Phase 1 — Concept screen: 3 distinct occasion ideas, light but complete asset sets. Placements open, broad audiences. Keep budgets even; look for clear separation on first-purchase CPA and hold-out AOV.
- Phase 2 — Asset breadth on the top 1–2 ideas: Add creator variants, alt hooks, and additional aspect ratios. Kill micro-edits that don’t change the story.
- Phase 3 — Page intent split: For the winning idea, run plain vs proof-heavy vs chooser page head-to-head. Keep message scent identical from ad to page; compare sCR, CPA, and AOV.
- Guardrails: Event fires once; purchase attribution reconciles with orders; watch latency on cAPI; avoid manual placement exclusions unless there’s brand safety risk.

- Home goods nuance: Campaign pages beat homepages when aligned to room/collection/offer; but for higher-ticket or custom, a plain promo page underperforms unless shoppers can compare or understand delivery/assembly.
