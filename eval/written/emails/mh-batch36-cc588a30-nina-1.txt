Valuation banding and confidence logic (transparent, non‑algorithmic feel)
- Banding
  • Take the internal numeric estimate and round to sensible anchors (e.g., nearest £5k or £10k) and present as a range.
  • Default range width: 4–6% of mid-point for Standard confidence; narrow to ~3% for High; widen to 8–10% for Low.
  • Never show a single exact figure; ranges reduce perceived false precision and compliance risk.

- Confidence (deterministic UI rules; no “AI” language)
  • High when: postcode + full address resolved, property type + bedrooms provided.
  • Standard when: postcode + property type present; bedrooms optional.
  • Low when: missing type/bedrooms, unusual property category, or out-of-area postcode.
  • Copy avoids model claims; keep it human: “based on typical homes nearby.”

- Privacy/compliance hygiene
  • Do not display or transmit full address with the price until consent; display area only (e.g., outward postcode like “CV32”).
  • Keep the fee banner visible pre-fields. Keep the “Guide only” line locked to the price panel.
  • If the range exceeds a threshold (e.g., >10%), prompt: “This range is wide — a quick in‑person appraisal will confirm.”

- valuation_booked
  Trigger: confirmed appraisal booking (online or logged from CRM sync).
