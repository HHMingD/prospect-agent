You said: Q4 corporate gifting is converting at 1.2%, CPCs spike, and you’re losing leads to calls/email with a 2–3 day proposal delay. Below is a tight teardown with 3 page fixes and 2 B2B ad angles you can use now. No fluff.

Where the page leaks today
- Above‑the‑fold is generic (“Say thank you with wine”), so ad traffic doesn’t see a single promise tied to Q4 needs.
- Friction: minimum 20 gifts + spreadsheet upload + 2–3 day proposal + payment only after approval. No instant quote or order, so urgency dies and people bail to phone/email.
- Mixed pricing signals: “Bottles from £12–£38 · Mixed cases from £65,” but no clear tiers to anchor budgets.

3 page fixes (fast, specific)
1) Bundled hero + reserve slot
- Replace the generic hero with a single‑promise module that matches your offer specifics.
- Pasteable hero copy:
  - Headline: Corporate Christmas wine gifts, UK‑wide
  - Sub: Minimum 20 gifts. From £12 per bottle. Branded note cards included. Delivery in 5–10 working days after payment.
  - Primary CTA: Get instant VAT‑quoted proposal (PDF)
  - Secondary CTA: Reserve a November–December delivery window
  - Tertiary link: Fewer than 20? Shop mixed cases
- Add three visible “bundle” tiles (not price lists, just decision anchors) that map to common budgets; beneath each, show what changes (producer tier, add‑ons) and reiterate lead time and minimum 20.

2) Instant quote with VAT‑itemised PDF (replaces the 2–3 day wait)
- On click of the primary CTA, show a short step‑one form (see fix 3). On submit, generate a branded PDF proposal instantly with:
  - Line items: e.g., quantity × bottle/mixed case, add‑ons (hamper, chocolate/cheese), branded note cards (included).
  - Totals: subtotal (ex VAT), VAT, courier/shipping, total due.
  - Delivery: “Lead time 5–10 working days after payment.”
  - Notes: “Prices exclude VAT and courier.” “Delivery windows 1 Nov – 20 Dec.” “Minimum order 20 gifts.”
  - Next step copy on the PDF footer: “Approve by reply to receive invoice the same day.”
- This lets procurement forward a VAT‑clear proposal internally the minute they’re warm, without waiting for your team.

3) Split the form: capture intent first, spreadsheet later
- Move the heavy lift (recipient spreadsheet) to step two, after they’ve got the instant PDF.
- Step‑one (above the fold or modal):
  - Full name, Company, Work email, Phone (optional), Number of recipients (min 20), Budget band, Preferred delivery window.
  - Microcopy under the upload: “No spreadsheet now? We’ll collect addresses after approval.”
- After submit, show: “Download your VAT‑itemised proposal (PDF)” + reminder of next steps. Then invite spreadsheet upload as an optional second step.
- Keep phone/email available in FAQ, but don’t present them as parallel CTAs in the hero—channel the click into the quote flow first to reduce leakage.
