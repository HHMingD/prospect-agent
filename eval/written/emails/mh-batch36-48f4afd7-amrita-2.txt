Launch checklist for a clean GEM‑era test

  utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign={{concept}}&utm_content={{format}}_{{ratio}}_{{version}}&utm_term={{page}}

Amrita —

Great to hear you’re building. A few launch‑week guardrails to keep the read clean and fast:

Structure
- 1 ad set per concept (Mud Wins, Rain Test), 3 creative variants each; even budget split; ~10% homepage holdout for baseline.
- Placements: keep Facebook, Instagram, Reels and Stories in the same ad set rather than splitting by surface. Meta’s new unified GEM model optimizes across these surfaces; a single cross‑surface setup avoids boxing learning in.

Naming + UTMs
- Campaign: sns_2026q4_concept-{rain|mud}
- Ad: var-{video|macro|carousel}-v1
- UTM: utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid-social&utm_campaign=concept-{rain|mud}&utm_content={video|macro|carousel}-v1&utm_term={page_slug}

Early kill/keep rules
- Day 3–5: pause any variant that’s bottom‑quartile on both CTR and first‑screen engagement (scroll or click to size selector).
- End of week 2: keep the lowest CPA‑by‑page concept; swap in one fresh variant under it; retire anything with three weeks of falling CTR.

Page first screen (copy‑paste checklist)
- Proof visual; single‑line promise; one reassurance (returns/delivery); primary CTA.
- Fit helper microcopy: “Age X–Y | ~H–H cm | True to size” (adjust to your chart).

Two fresh micro‑tests
- Wording: “Reinforced knees” vs “Double‑layer knees” in the hero.
- Price overlay on carousel: on vs off.

Housekeeping
- Make sure “first order” is attributed by landing page (not collapsed into homepage by redirects).
- For paid landers, hide the main nav to reduce leaks; add a compact “Back to shop” link under the hero.

Meta announced GEM on 13 Oct; expect a little week‑one noise. Use relative gaps for the first cuts; let week‑two CPA decide the keepers.
