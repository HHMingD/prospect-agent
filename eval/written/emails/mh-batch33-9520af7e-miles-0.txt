60‑second ROAS line
1) For a product with price R and gross margin m, max ad spend A = (m × R) − variable costs (shipping, payments, assembly, returns).
2) Minimum target ROAS = R ÷ A. If A ≤ 0, don’t advertise that SKU for delivery; push click‑and‑collect.

Cannibalisation quick test (30 minutes)
- Split brand vs non‑brand: keep an exact‑match brand Search campaign and exclude brand terms in PMAX so Shopping isn’t rebuying brand clicks.
