Formulas (paste into row 2 and copy down)
- L2 (R1_rate): =IF(D2=0,0,F2/D2)
- M2 (R2_rate): =IF(F2=0,0,G2/F2)
- N2 (Rev90d_per_acq): =IF(D2=0,0,(H2/D2) + L2*(I2/D2) + (L2*M2)*(J2/D2))
- O2 (Payback90d): =N2 - IF(D2=0,0,K2/D2)

Reading lift (example)
- Uplift in 90‑day revenue per acquired: =(Variant_Rev90d_per_acq − Holdout_Rev90d_per_acq) / Holdout_Rev90d_per_acq
- R1 rate delta: =Variant_R1_rate − Holdout_R1_rate
Optional significance check on R1 (per cohort or pooled):
- SE_variant = SQRT(Variant_R1_rate*(1−Variant_R1_rate)/Variant_trials)
- SE_holdout = SQRT(Holdout_R1_rate*(1−Holdout_R1_rate)/Holdout_trials)
- z = (R1_delta) / SQRT(SE_variant^2 + SE_holdout^2)
