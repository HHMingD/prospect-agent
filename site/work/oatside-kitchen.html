Oatside Kitchen

Offer page three: the plainest one.

Cost per first order down 38%

Oatside Kitchen's ads were good. They all sent people to the homepage, where the offer from the ad was three scrolls down.

We moved the ads off the homepage onto three offer pages, one for each thing the ads promised, and let the ads find their page.

Cost per first order came down 38%.
