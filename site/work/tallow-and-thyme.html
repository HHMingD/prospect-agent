Tallow & Thyme

The gift finder, Christmas week.

Christmas paid revenue +74%

Their first Christmas ads sent people to a grid of 60 candles. Most left. We built a three-question gift finder in four days and pointed every gifting ad at it.

Three questions: who is it for, which smell do they love, and how much do you want to spend. Every answer narrowed 60 candles to three.

The finder converted at 4.8% against 1.9% for the grid.
