Stop sending ads to your homepage

One ad, one promise, one page.

A homepage is written for everyone, so it speaks to no one in particular. Someone who clicked an ad about one product lands on a page about all of them, and has to find their way back to the thing they clicked.

About half gone in five seconds

In our audits, homepages lose about half of paid traffic in the first five seconds.

A page built for one ad keeps the promise the ad made, in the first screen, with one thing to do next. It is cheaper to build than most people think: often a day or two, not a redesign.
