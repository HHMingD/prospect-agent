When your numbers disagree

Meta says one thing, Google says another, and your shop says a third. Most brands we audit cannot tell which ad paid for itself.

That is why the first two weeks of every engagement are an audit: ad account, tracking, landing pages and the last 12 months of spend. Half of what looks like an ads problem turns out to be a counting problem.

Count the thing you want, not the click

Count what you actually want: a sale, a first order, a booked demo, not a click.

Once the counting is right, the decisions get boring, in a good way. Budget goes to the terms and audiences that produce the thing you counted, and away from the ones that only produce reports.
