The three-week creative rule

We retire most creative after about three weeks of falling click-through.

Not three weeks from launch: three weeks of falling. An ad that is still climbing stays, however old it is. An ad that has slid for three weeks rarely comes back, and every week it runs, it teaches the account less.

Three weeks of falling click-through

What replaces it is not one new ad. Every concept we make ships with at least three variants, so the account can learn which one works rather than us guessing.

The rule only works if there is always something in the queue. That is the real discipline: making creative on a schedule, before the old ads tire, not after.
