Why your Meta costs went up

Rising costs usually come from creative fatigue and broad targeting fighting thin creative, not the auction alone.

When an account runs four ads for six months, the algorithm has nothing new to learn from. It keeps showing the same people the same thing, and each impression buys less.

Same four ads since spring

The fix is rarely a bigger budget. It is more to test: new concepts, each in at least three variants, so the account can find out which one works.

Look at your ads manager before you look at your bids. If the newest ad in it is older than your last season, that is your cost problem.
